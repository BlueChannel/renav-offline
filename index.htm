RENAV Has Closed

After 18 years of serving Colorado's real estate community, RENAV officially ceased operations on October 31, 2026.

We want to sincerely thank our customers, partners, and colleagues for their support and trust over the years. It's been a privilege to provide data and research tools to Colorado's real estate professionals.

Looking for Colorado Foreclosure Data?

Unfortunately, we're not aware of a single third-party provider offering the same comprehensive foreclosure information previously available through RENAV.

For those seeking similar information, we recommend obtaining foreclosure records directly from Colorado county Public Trustee offices and supplementing those records with information from county Clerk and Recorder offices.
